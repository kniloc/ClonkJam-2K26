## How to Play

1. Move your mouse to aim the bat.
2. Watch the incoming baseball and line up your swing.
3. Click the mouse to swing.
4. Hit the ball when it reaches the target area.
5. If you miss, you get a strike.
6. Get three strikes and you lose.
7. Hit the ball successfully to score a home run and win.
